# Artifact

## Definition
An Artifact is the final output produced by a [[concepts/tool|Tool]] after processing data through its sequence of [[concepts/workflow-step|WorkflowStep]] operations. It represents the realization and culmination of a tool's primary function, frequently generating specific [[concepts/asset|Asset]] types such as briefs, brand voice guidelines, or personas.

## Key Characteristics
- **Final Deliverable**: Serves as the ultimate outcome generated by a tool's processing lifecycle.
- **Workflow Integration**: Produced as the final result following the sequential execution of one or more [[concepts/workflow-step|WorkflowStep]] phases.
- **Asset Instantiation**: Directly maps to or generates concrete [[concepts/asset|Asset]] types (e.g., 'brief', 'brand-voice', and 'persona').
- **Goal Realization**: Acts as the functional proof of completion for the overarching purpose of a tool.

## Applications
- **Asset Generation**: Structuring and delivering domain-specific deliverables like persona profiles, messaging strategies, or brand voice documents.
- **Tool Pipeline Orchestration**: Serving as the final output payload that can be ingested by downstream systems or human stakeholders.
